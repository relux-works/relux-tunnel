The first, second, third, and fifth components carry identical MIT license text
and the same "Copyright (c) 2022 hev" notice in their pinned license files. They
are aggregated below without changing the notice or permission text. The lwIP
license is reproduced separately and in full.

The root source tree also vendors Wintun files under third-part/wintun. The
Apple build does not merge the Windows DLL into HevSocks5Tunnel.xcframework,
and the Windows-only C branches compile to no-symbol objects on Apple. Wintun is
therefore not included in this Apple binary notice sample. If a Windows artifact
is ever distributed, its pinned LICENSE.txt must be reviewed and included under
that artifact's separate compliance scope.
