# Record the approved M0 SSH engine selection

## Description
Record the binding M0 SSH engine ADR from the human-approved 2026-07-28 decision plus audited build, conformance, functional, compatibility, client-rekey, cancellation, and lifecycle evidence gathered on the macOS harness. libssh2 is the approved primary candidate; ReluxNIOSSH is retained as recorded comparative evidence and receives no further fork work unless new evidence invalidates libssh2. Physical scale, memory, and deep-observability numbers stay explicit M3 evidence gates and must not be asserted here.

## Scope
In scope: the approved decision and its rationale; candidate versions and exact dependency pins; adapter boundary; approved algorithms; pre-auth host verification; auth; direct-tcpip; exec and exec/stdin upload; client-rekey trigger; cancellation and lifecycle; bounded-memory posture measured on the macOS harness; license and security-monitoring posture; fork and upstream-rebase owner; the four M3-deferred semantics with their owner task; residual risks; revalidation triggers; downstream readiness for M1 and M2. Out of scope: asserting physical-hardware scale or extension-budget numbers not yet measured, waiving an M0-viability-mandatory gate, reopening the approved decision without new evidence, NIOSSH fork work, lane scheduling, relay protocol, profile UX, SFTP, ProxyJump, and release approval.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. A TASK-ID-scoped ADR maps every M0-viability-mandatory gate to row-level evidence and marks pass, fail, or not run with reason for the selected engine, and preserves the recorded ReluxNIOSSH comparative evidence. 2. The selected engine passes every M0-viability-mandatory gate, including macOS target integration, pre-auth host evidence, approved auth, concurrent direct-tcpip channels achievable on the harness, long-lived exec, client-rekey trigger, real relux-server compatibility, bounded memory, and leak-free lifecycle. 3. A red mandatory gate routes selection back to analysis or to the alternate candidate; no mandatory threshold is weakened after results are known. 4. The ADR records exact dependency pins, adapter boundary, approved algorithms, initial window and rekey policy ranges as injectable values, license notices, security monitoring, and the fork or upstream-rebase owner. 5. The four M3-deferred semantics, the physical scale/memory evidence still owed, rejected evidence, residual risks, revalidation triggers, and the downstream tasks unblocked for M1 and M2 are explicit.
