## A0 decoupling decision

Date: 2026-07-28

For the current objective — a working macOS protocol hypothesis and client — authoritative Apple intended-use evidence (Gate A0) is not a critical-path prerequisite. Core, harness, generated project, provider implementation, and local physical validation may proceed without it.

A0 remains a later distribution/compliance risk. No task may claim Apple approval, App Store acceptance, or entitlement approval without independent evidence.

Gate P0 remains separately required for a signed packet-tunnel extension on the available Apple-silicon Mac.
